Household Assistant — Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Program name

Household Assistant.

Program description

Household Assistant is a private, personal text-message service operated by an individual for members of his own household. It sends schedule and deadline reminders to a household member, accepts replies conversationally, and sends occasional family coordination messages (for example, a reminder about a family dinner) to members of the operator's immediate family.

It is not a commercial service. It is not offered to the public, it has no customers, and nobody outside the operator's own household can enrol in it.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Recipients typically receive between one and five messages per day. Family coordination messages are sent no more than a few times per month.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Household Assistant does not charge recipients anything. Any charges are those of the recipient's own mobile carrier.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. Opting out is immediate and permanent until the recipient opts back in. There is no penalty and no follow-up message asking the recipient to reconsider.

The words STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT all stop messages.

Getting help

Reply HELP at any time for information about the service and how to contact the operator. You can also email t.josh.pratt+sms@gmail.com.

Carrier liability

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Supported carriers

Major United States carriers, including AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon and their affiliates.

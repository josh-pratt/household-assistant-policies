Household Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Who operates this service

Household Assistant is a private text-message service operated by an individual for members of his own household. It is not a business, has no customers, and is not offered to the public.

What information is collected

Only what is necessary to operate the service:

  • The mobile phone numbers of household members who have agreed to receive messages.
  • The content of messages sent to and from the service.
  • Schedule information, deadlines and reminders that a household member chooses to send to the service or authorises it to read on her behalf.

How the information is used

Solely to deliver the reminders and coordination messages the recipient has asked for. Nothing else.

What is never done with it

  • It is never sold, rented or traded.
  • It is never shared with third parties for their own purposes.
  • It is never used for marketing of any kind, by the operator or anyone else.
  • No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • It is not used to build advertising profiles.

Service providers

Messages are delivered through a licensed communications provider, which processes them only to transmit them. Mobile carriers necessarily handle messages in order to deliver them. No other party receives this data.

Retention

Message history is retained only as long as it is useful to the recipient, and is deleted on request.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop all messages immediately. Reply HELP for assistance.

Children

The service is operated within a single household and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Household Assistant

Text-message appointment and due-date reminders

Household Assistant is a private text-message reminder service operated by Thomas Joshua Pratt, a sole proprietor, for himself and adult members of his own immediate household. It sends a daily summary of the recipient's own calendar appointments and coursework due dates, alerts the recipient when one of those changes, and replies to messages the recipient sends to it.

There is no marketing, no promotional content, and no third-party or affiliate content. Messages are informational only.

Sign up for text reminders

To sign up, text the word START to +1 (385) 398-9120 from the mobile phone you want reminders on.

You will receive one confirmation message, and reminders will begin. Texting START from your own phone is what gives consent, and it is the only way to join.

By texting START you agree to the following:

I agree to receive recurring informational text messages from Thomas Joshua Pratt about my appointments, coursework due dates and reminders. Message frequency varies, up to 20 messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe and HELP for help. See our Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Consent to receive text messages is not required to use any service and is not a condition of any purchase. Texting START is entirely voluntary. No message is ever sent to a number that has not texted START, and this site does not collect, transmit or store any phone number.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help. Opting out is immediate. There is no penalty and no message asking you to reconsider.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies, up to 20 messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Household Assistant does not charge recipients anything; any charges are those of your own mobile carrier.
